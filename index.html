Good to know

A few little answers.

Can the invitation be personalised?

Yes. Names, dates, venue details, event schedule, wording, colours and selected design elements can be personalised.

Will guests need to install an app?

No. Your digital invitation is shared as a link and can be opened in a browser on a phone.

How long does it take?

Turnaround depends on the design and the details you need. We’ll confirm the timeline with your quote.

Can I see a preview before final delivery?

Yes. A watermarked preview can be shared for review. Final unwatermarked files or links are delivered after approval and payment as agreed.
